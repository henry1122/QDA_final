Notes:
  pp(k) = PhasePoly A* with joint synthesis (Stage 6):
          k=1: per-block independent A*
          k>1: blocks partitioned into groups of k; each consecutive pair within
               a group is synthesized jointly (a single CNOT can reduce parity
               columns from BOTH blocks simultaneously); trailing singleton
               within a group is synthesized independently.
          Infeasible pairs (H-boundary singularity) and budget-exhausted pairs
          fall back to independent A* — so pp(k>1) >= pp(k=1) is impossible.

  todd+naive = full-circuit Todd T-count opt (Tableau pipeline) + naive rotation synthesis
